OVERVIEW

Color The Most Tiles Before Time Runs Out!

It's Me Pico is a portrait-mode hyper-casual territory control game where players compete to paint more grid tiles than their opponent. Earn coins from your matches, unlock quirky cosmetic characters, discover mystical floating arenas, and conquer your way through Pico's colorful world.
