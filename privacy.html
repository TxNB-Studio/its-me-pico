Privacy Policy

At TXnB Studio, we build mobile games for fun, experimentation, and self-learning. We value your privacy and believe in keeping things transparent. This policy outlines how "It's Me Pico" handles user data.

1. Data We Do Not Collect

"It's Me Pico" is designed to run locally on your device. We do not run custom servers, force account creation, or collect personal information.

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your device's contacts, photos, or file storage beyond local game files.

2. Local Storage

Your game progress, including your coin balance, unlocked characters, high scores, and arena states, is stored locally on your mobile device (using device-specific secure sandboxed storage). Removing the application will delete this local progress, as we do not sync state to external cloud servers.

3. Device Identifiers & Analytics

To help us improve the game and support independent development, the game may use default mobile platform services (such as Google Play Services or Apple Game Center) to track achievements and display leaderboards. These platforms may collect standardized hardware IDs and usage data in accordance with their respective privacy settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Since our game does not collect personal information or request data entry, it is safe for players of all ages. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please reach out to us at our contact email, and we will take immediate action to delete it.
